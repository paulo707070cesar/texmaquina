## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-reliability industrial engineering distilled into an intuitive digital experience. Designed specifically for industrial textile machinery asset management, procurement, and operations, the aesthetic merges the crisp discipline of heavy machinery architecture with the fluidity of modern enterprise cloud software.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Engineered Reliability:** Conveys structural durability, zero tolerance for error, and operational rigor.
- **Luminous Precision:** Uses airy light canvases, precise geometry, and focused high-clarity teal highlights to elevate technical data without visual fatigue.
- **Intelligent Velocity:** Interactions feel instantaneous and well-lubricated, matching high-throughput manufacturing demands.

### Design Movement & Execution
The aesthetic relies on **Technical Glassmorphism & Precision Minimalism**. Crisp structural lines, hairline separation borders, and diffused light refraction mimic the polished steel, clear telemetry displays, and calibrated dials of modern textile looms. Soft ambient backdrops paired with frosted container layers provide deep context while maintaining dense data readability.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces industrial instrumentation under natural studio light. Primary accents anchor attention on mission-critical CTAs and system telemetry, while carefully calibrated status tokens ensure instant operational awareness on the manufacturing floor and in executive suites.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0096C7`):** Electric Technical Cyan. Utilized for primary interactive touchpoints, confirmed states, active telemetry plots, and key conversions.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`):** Deep Forged Slate. Anchors structural frames, high-level headers, and high-contrast control surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Focused Hydraulic Azure. Secondary interactions, active toggle states, and linked asset tags.
- **Neutral Surface & Canvas (`#F4F7F9`):** Soft Cool Base. A calibrated non-fatiguing backdrop providing high chromatic contrast for white machinery cards.
- **Card Fill (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure optical white with hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) and diffused atmospheric shadows.

### Status Semantics (PT-BR Manufacturing Context)
- **Operacional / Em Estoque (Success):** Foreground `#059669`, Badge Background `#D1FAE5`, Border `#A7F3D0`.
- **Manutenção / Em Negociação / Reservado (Warning):** Foreground `#D97706`, Badge Background `#FEF3C7`, Border `#FDE68A`.
- **Interrupção / Crítico / Vendido (Error):** Foreground `#DC2626`, Badge Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `#FECACA`.
- **Inativo / Standby (Neutral):** Foreground `#475569`, Badge Background `#F1F5F9`, Border `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured geometry of `Manrope` for display and headline levels with the rapid legibility of `Hanken Grotesk` for data density and body text. 

### Typographic Governance
- **Manrope:** Geometric proportions convey high-precision machinery calibration. Use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on larger headings to produce authoritative, compact titles.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Neutral, highly legible grotesque characteristics ensure machinery serial numbers, RPM counters, and financial transactions read cleanly without confusion between similar glyphs.
- **Numerical Telemetry:** When rendering production capacity, pricing, and serial codes, apply tabular numeric figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shift during realtime telemetry updates.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an 8pt structural baseline paired with a 12-column fluid grid on desktop viewports and a single/double-column adaptive stack on mobile devices.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer page margins, `1.5rem` structural gutters. Side navigation remains persistent, with data grids spanning full container widths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins. Filter sidebars collapse into high-speed slide-over drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margins. Touch targets maintain a minimum vertical height of `48px`. Critical controls stick to lower-screen thumb-zones.

### Spacing Discipline
Internal component layout follows strict semantic grouping:
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text gaps, badge padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Stacked input field labels and validation messages.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal card body spacing, row gap in table lists.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-card gap in machinery showcases and metrics grids.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section divisions between operational overviews and inventory tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on light refraction and layered translucency rather than heavy dark drop shadows. Surfaces reflect a spotless manufacturing cleanroom.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F4F7F9`):** Ground zero. Non-elevated foundation.
- **Frosted Control Tier (Glassmorphism):** Sticky header bars, telemetry filters, and sub-nav modules utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `16px` and a crisp lower hairline border of `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Card Surface Level 1:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Card Hover / Active Inspection Level 2:** Triggered during asset card hover. Surface elevates with border color shifting to `#0096C7 / 30%`, shadow `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 150, 199, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Modal & Diagnostic Overlays Level 3:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`. Backdrop dimmed with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)` blended with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs sweeping, pill-like contours contrasted against hyper-precise inner data grids. This balance softens industrial coldness while retaining a disciplined technical edge.

### Radius Mapping
- **Interactive Badges, Chips, Pills:** Full capsule geometry (`9999px`).
- **Primary Buttons & Form Inputs (`rounded`):** `1rem` (16px) radius, providing a tactile, modern touch target.
- **Machinery Cards & Metric Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `2rem` (32px) radius, conveying unified structural containment.
- **Hero Containers & Modular Dashboards (`rounded-xl`):** `3rem` (48px) radius on outer wrapper surfaces.
- **Data Tables & Telemetry Matrices:** Outer frames inherit `rounded-lg`, while internal cells retain strict orthogonal borders for alignment accuracy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Background `#0096C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, radius `1rem`. Active state scales down to `0.98` with background `#0284C7`. Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(0, 150, 199, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `1rem`. Hover: border `#0096C7`, text `#0096C7`, background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent fill, text `#64748B`. Hover: text `#0F172A`, background `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.6)`.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Capsule format (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Em Estoque:** Background `#D1FAE5`, text `#059669`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Includes a preceding pulsating dot indicator.
- **Reservado / Negociação:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#D97706`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Vendido:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Machinery Spec Cards
- Outer border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, radius `2rem`.
- Upper card zone houses technical photography or 3D schematics with subtle zoom transition on container hover (`scale: 1.02`).
- Lower zone houses machinery telemetry (e.g., Modelo, RPM Máximo, Ano, Status, Preço) rendered via structured micro-grids with `border-t border-[#F1F5F9]`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height `44px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile). Radius `1rem`.
- Default: Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, typography `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- Focused: Border `1.5px solid #0096C7`, shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 150, 199, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base dimension `20px x 20px`. Checkbox radius `6px`, radio radius `9999px`.
- Inactive: Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Selected: Background `#0096C7`, border `#0096C7`, centered check or dot in `#FFFFFF`. Smooth spring scale animation on state toggle.

### Machinery Telemetry Lists (Data Tables)
- Frosted header row with sticky alignment, background `rgba(244, 247, 249, 0.95)`, text uppercase `label-sm` in `#64748B`.
- Alternate row striping avoided; separation delivered via `1px solid #F1F5F9` bottom divider.
- Hovered row shifts to background `#F8FAFC` with left edge accent mark in `#0096C7`.
